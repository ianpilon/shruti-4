A synthesized recreation of the Aerospace Atmosphere pads, built from measurements of their demo loops rather than samples: root and fifth in every tonality, a faint second for Major and a faint minor third for Minor, a low-pass ceiling near 1 kHz, separate left and right voices that drift apart, and loudness swells of 6 to 30 seconds. Each layer is a different register and voicing, as in the original: Stratosphere and Thermosphere are bass, Troposphere and Mesosphere mid, Exosphere the only bright one. Space engages and aborts, ← → change key, ↑ ↓ change layer.
