Switch picks the drone for all four boxes, one at a time. Pump sets how fast that box breathes. Finger on the strip shapes its voice: low is dark and soft, high is bright and full. It stays where you leave it. Hold freezes a box's breath where it is. Lock makes Box 1 the clock and snaps the other dials to fractions of it. The dropdown under each box name picks its voice: the shruti reed, or one of five pad voices (Bed, Deep, Haze, Pulse, Glow), all played through the same bellows and knobs. 8vb drops a box an octave for a bass voice. Age is how out of tune the four boxes are with each other, Width how far apart each reed's left and right halves sit, Breath how deep every pump stroke is, Level the master volume. Drive is warmth up to half and real distortion above it, biting harder with more air. Grit folds the wave, adds presence and opens the ceiling: the harsh, nasty end. Room sends that box into a shared reverb. Level is that box's place in the mix, Pan its place left to right. Presets saves the whole panel, boxes and holds included. H holds all, L toggles lock, Space stops all.
