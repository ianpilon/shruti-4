SHRUTI 4 model SB-4 · reed & pad droneNo presets: the panel is the sound. Switch picks the drone for all four boxes, one at a time. Pump sets how fast that box breathes. Finger on the strip shapes its voice: low is dark and soft, high is bright and full. It stays where you leave it. The short squeeze ribbon beside it is momentary: touch and slide up to push extra air, let go and it settles back, even while held. Turn Pump all the way down and the breath stops where it is: that is hold. In Locked mode, Box 1's Pump at the bottom holds all four. Lock makes Box 1 the clock and snaps the other dials to fractions of it. The rotary switch under each box name picks its voice: the shruti reed, or one of five pad voices (Bed, Deep, Haze, Pulse, Glow), all played through the same bellows and knobs. 8vb drops a box an octave for a bass voice. Age is how out of tune the four boxes are with each other, Width how far apart each reed's left and right halves sit, Breath how deep every pump stroke is, Level the master volume. Drive is warmth up to half and real distortion above it, biting harder with more air. Grit folds the wave, adds presence and opens the ceiling: the harsh, nasty end. Each box has an off / on toggle; the lever position is the only indicator, as on a real switch. Room sends that box into a shared reverb. Level is that box's place in the mix, Pan its place left to right. L toggles lock, Space stops all.
