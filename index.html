You can damage your hearing or your speakers if you play tones at extreme volumes. Set the frequency to 1,000 Hz, find a volume level that is comfortable, and keep the volume near that level while you explore other frequencies. Very low and very high tones sound quiet even at high volume, so do not turn the volume up to compensate.
